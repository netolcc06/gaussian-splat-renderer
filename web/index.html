About

C++ compiled to WebAssembly (Emscripten) with a WebGPU compute tile rasterizer, a port of a Vulkan 3D Gaussian Splatting renderer. Rendering follows Kerbl et al., 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering (SIGGRAPH 2023).

Demo scene: “Knock Community Hall” by scbenoit (source), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Modified: converted from SOG to 3DGS PLY and decimated to 300,000 gaussians (details).

WebGPU via Emscripten’s emdawnwebgpu port (Dawn, BSD-3-Clause).
